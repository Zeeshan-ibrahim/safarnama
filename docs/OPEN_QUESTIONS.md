# Open questions — do not silently assume

## Product
1. Which pilot cluster: Hunza–Gilgit, Skardu–Shigar–Khaplu, Naran–Kaghan, or another?
2. Exact dates, approximate month/season, or both?
3. Anonymous draft first, login only to save/share?
4. How should total-group vs per-person budget and inclusions be captured?
5. First beachhead: friends/couples, families, or first-time domestic travellers?
6. Minimum source quality and freshness standards for routes, costs, seasonality, and attractions?

## Data/integrations
7. Google Maps vs Mapbox for Pakistan coverage, cost, accuracy, and licensing?
8. Which trusted sources/local partners verify destination and road data?
9. How will fuel, stays, tolls, tickets, and activity costs be refreshed?
10. Which LLM provider is approved and what data may be sent?

## Engineering
11. What does the actual repository already implement? Confirm stack, deployment, and CI.
12. Which authentication approach?
13. What privacy, retention, and account-deletion rules?
14. Which analytics and observability tools?

Do not block unrelated foundations on every open question. Use clean interfaces and explicit unsupported states.
