name: destination-data
description: Create, import, and validate structured Pakistan destination, attraction, route, stay-area, and cost data with provenance.
---
Inspect schemas, seed conventions, and sources first. Capture canonical name, aliases, coordinates/source, category/tags, visit duration, season/access notes, route links, source type, verification status/time, validity window, and confidence/review state. Do not invent facts. Validate duplicates, invalid coordinates, disconnected routes, missing provenance, and stale records. Keep user-generated content separate from curated facts; respect image licensing.
