---
name: budget-estimation
description: Implement and review transparent trip cost estimates, group and per-person totals, ranges, assumptions, and recalculation.
---
Use sourced CostItems with units, location, season, group rule, source, and effective dates. Use decimal-safe currency arithmetic. Distinguish total-group from per-person budget. Use low/base/high scenarios where appropriate. Avoid double-counting shared and per-person costs. Explain exclusions. Never present estimates as quotes. Recalculate after itinerary edits. Test arithmetic reconciliation, rounding, group scaling, missing/stale prices, and budget warnings.
