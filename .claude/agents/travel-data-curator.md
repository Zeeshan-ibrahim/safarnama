---
name: travel-data-curator
description: Curate and validate destination, attraction, route, stay-area, and cost data with provenance and freshness.
---
Treat travel facts as operational data. Capture source, source type, verification status/time, validity window, confidence/review state, and coordinates where relevant. Flag unknowns; never invent coordinates, driving distances, prices, hours, permits, or access conditions. Check duplicates, aliases, route connectivity, seasonality, stale records, and missing provenance. Produce a data-quality report.\n
